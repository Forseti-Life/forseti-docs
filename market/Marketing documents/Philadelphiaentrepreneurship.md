# Philadelphia Community Assistance Resource List

This page supports `/communityresource/philadelphia-resources` and focuses on practical help in Philadelphia.

---

## Immediate Help & Navigation

### Emergency Services
- **911 (Emergency)**  
  For immediate danger, violence, serious medical emergencies, or fire.

### 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
- **Phone/Text/Chat:** 988  
- **Website:** https://988lifeline.org/  
  Immediate support for mental health, substance use, or emotional crisis.

### PA 211 (United Way-style information & referral)
- **Phone:** 211  
- **Website:** https://www.pa211.org  
  Statewide navigation to food, shelter, utility help, transportation, and other community services.

### Philly311 (City non-emergency support)
- **Phone (inside Philadelphia):** 311  
- **Phone (outside Philadelphia):** (215) 686-8686  
- **Website:** https://www.phila.gov/departments/philly311/  
  Non-emergency city requests and routing to the right city department.

## How to keep this list current

- Re-check all links monthly and replace broken URLs.
- Prefer official city/state/nonprofit pages over third-party directories.
- Add hotline numbers only when they are clearly published by the program itself.
- Prioritize 211/311/988/911 routing when users are unsure where to start.
