## Goal

Add Ratfolk as a playable ancestry with its Small stat block, low-light vision, and heritages for quadrupedal movement bursts, scent, disease resilience, social-animal coercion, cold resistance, and improved squeezing.

## Source reference

> Ratfolk are Uncommon Small ancestries with HP 6, Speed 25, Dexterity/Intelligence/Free boosts, a Strength flaw, and low-light vision.

## Implementation hint

Create the ratfolk ancestry plus heritage records for Desert, Longsnout, Sewer, Shadow, Snow, and Tunnel ratfolk. The implementation needs conditional movement changes, disease and poison stage adjustment logic, animal-attitude modifiers, and environmental resilience rules that scale by level or terrain.
