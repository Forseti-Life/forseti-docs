## Goal
Add a brief statement on the Dungeoncrawler home page informing players that community suggestions
are being reviewed and implemented. This closes the feedback loop and signals that the platform
is responsive to its users — directly supporting the community-managed mission.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A visible message or banner appears on the dungeoncrawler home page
- [ ] The message communicates that player suggestions are actively reviewed and implemented
- [ ] The message links to or mentions where suggestions can be submitted
- [ ] No existing layout or functionality is broken
- [ ] Change is committed to the dungeoncrawler codebase

## Notes
- Copy suggestion: e.g. "We build DungeonCrawler with your ideas — submit a suggestion and see it ship."
- Placement: below hero or in a sidebar/footer callout — PM/Dev discretion on exact placement
