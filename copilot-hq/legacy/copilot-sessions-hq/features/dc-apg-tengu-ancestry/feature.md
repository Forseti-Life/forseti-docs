## Goal

Add Tengu as a playable ancestry with its Sharp Beak natural attack and heritage options for anti-curse luck, anti-undead magic, total falling immunity, storm resilience, and talon attacks.

## Source reference

> Tengu are Uncommon Medium ancestries with HP 6, Speed 25, Dexterity plus free boosts, low-light vision, and a default beak unarmed attack.

## Implementation hint

Create tengu ancestry data plus heritage records for Jinxed, Mountainkeeper, Skyborn, Stormtossed, and Taloned tengu. This needs support for save-upgrade reactions, concealment exceptions in weather, zero-damage falling rules, primal or divine innate cantrip grants, and multiple ancestry-provided unarmed attacks.
